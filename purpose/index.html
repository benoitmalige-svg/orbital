Purpose.

What do we do with a life we know will end?

If you've just finished The Identity Tax, this is where I'd invite you to continue.

Every month, I write a philosophical investigation for Les Essais, a private correspondence delivered in print to its members. Each issue is printed once, sent by mail, and never published in a public archive.

Purpose is one of those investigations. Across 30+ pages, I explore a question that becomes particularly difficult to ignore once we confront our own mortality.

I'd like to invite you to read the complete digital edition, privately, as a glimpse into the practice I've built around these questions. Read it slowly. Disagree with me. See where it takes you.
